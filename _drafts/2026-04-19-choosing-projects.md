## We've all been there

If you're a creative in any capacity, whether its for work or personal hobby, you've no doubt come across creative blocks of all kinds; it's pretty much inevitable whether its choice or analysis paralysis, rethinking things in the middle of it, doubts about the final product, etc etc. Today, I wanted to talk about my experiences, things that have/haven't worked for me, and have yet to find a consistent fix for. I'll be talking from the perspective of a personal hobby, but points can easily apply for professional work too.

### Disclaimers

I should mention a few things that I feel are pretty important to add additional context to the things I'm talking about.

* I am an "engineering" maker, which means I need to learn and apply lots of ME, EE, and CS things to my projects for them to be made
* I work a compressed schedule (minimum 3 day weekends)
* I contribute to multiple engineering/maker groups that take up most of my non-work schedule
* I have ADHD (been diagnosed since middle school and not medicated since then)
